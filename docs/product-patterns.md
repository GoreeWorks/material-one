# Material One Product Patterns

Material One patterns define how foundations, controls, and the application shell combine into complete experiences.

Patterns are adaptive structures rather than fixed page templates.

## Pattern families

- Landing pages for public websites and product discovery
- Dashboards for metrics, activity, and high-frequency actions
- Workspaces for professional and creative tools
- Catalogs for ecommerce, inventory, collections, and search
- Checkout flows for focused transactions
- Settings for preferences, themes, accessibility, and accounts
- Detail and master-detail layouts for information-heavy products

## Adaptation

Compact layouts prioritize reachability and sequential focus.

Expanded layouts add parallel information without reproducing full desktop density.

Workspace layouts use available area for persistent context, richer data, and simultaneous tasks.

Catalog filters should be persistent on large workspaces and move into a drawer on smaller layouts. Checkout experiences should reduce distraction and keep the primary action reachable.

Products may define their own content, imagery, brand accent, tone, and workflow while inheriting Material One semantic tokens, accessibility behavior, adaptation, and interaction language.
